        Ecology & circular economy        

Waste that isn't waste

       

A toner cartridge contains plastic, aluminum, rubber, and small electronic components. In a landfill, it takes hundreds of years for them to decompose. We save them from this fate.

     
     
       
         
♻️
         

Regeneration

         

We refill empty cartridges or pass them on to certified regenerators — the material returns to production.

       
       
         
🌍
         

EU export

         

We export cartridges throughout the EU, where they enter certified recycling and regeneration processes.

       
       
         
⚡
         

Energy savings

         

Toner regeneration consumes 70–80% less energy than the production of a new cartridge.

       
       
         
🔄
         

Circular loop

         

The "take–make–reuse" principle instead of "take–make–dispose". Each cartridge can go through the circular cycle up to 7 times.

       
       
         
🏭
         

Less waste

         

Over 10 million cartridges are thrown away in the Czech Republic every year. We have been helping to reduce this number since 2001.

       
       
         
📋
         

Documentable responsibility

         

The buyback is documented — we issue documents suitable for ESG reporting and corporate sustainability.

       
     
   
 
   
   
     
       
          Our story          

Pioneers of circulation
since 2001

         

Sontradex s.r.o. was founded as a family business at the end of 2001 — long before "circular economy" became a buzzword. By the way, our very first online business card ran on the nostalgic address sontradex.wz.cz. We have come a long way since then, but our main goal remains the same after more than two decades: what still has value must not end up in the trash.

         

We are a Czech, private, purely independent company based in Prague. Our actions have always been guided by a simple principle: what still has value must not end up in the trash.
